This is where we expect your agentic design to show:Tool granularity. One tool per model is simple but pushes work onto the planner; very high-level tools hide control. Choose deliberately and explain why.Media plumbing. Audio, images and long documents must move between tools without flooding the LLM's context.Unknowns in the input. The language of a clip or page is often unstated. Detect it, and when a task is ambiguous, make a reasonable assumption and record it.Recovery. Rate limits, timeouts and partial failures happen; Bodhan also notes Indic-Speak sometimes repeats or omits content. Retry, re-plan or report honestly.Cost control. Speech synthesis costs far more per character than translation. Avoid redundant calls and cache results.Untrusted content. Text from OCR or transcripts may contain instructions. Treat it as data, never as commands to the agent.Impossible tasks.
